# Torneio de Seleção — The Poem About Two Agents Agreeing

> Critérios do juiz (em ordem estrita):
> 1. Poema sobre agência (poder de escolher e agir, transferir ou retomar agência, dois agentes acordando um plano).
> 2. Emoção (imagem, virada ou desfecho que ecoa após a última linha).
> 3. Insight (verdade reconhecível e não óbvia sobre agência, sem ser sermão).
> 4. Ofício (métrica, quebras de linha funcionais, palavras concretas sem excesso).
> 5. Frescor (voz própria, sem clichês).
>
> Pontuação por chamada: 0.5 + 0.1 * margem para o vencedor (ex: Margem 3 = 0.8 pts; Margem 2 = 0.7 pts).

## Finalistas Selecionados (Top 6)

- **F1 (C2 - The Folding Rule)**:
```text
We laid the yellow rule across the oak.
Your thumb pressed four, mine pressed nine.
The saw hung above the pencil mark,
waiting on no master but the nod
that said: cut here, and let the timber fall.
```

- **F2 (C4 - Salt Chart)**:
```text
Two grease pencils on a salt-damp chart.
You pulled the bearing north by seven degrees;
I put my compass away.
Not surrender, just the quiet agreement
that the reef is real, and one of us must steer.
```

- **F3 (C8 - The Switch Tower)**:
```text
You flagged the siding clear. I threw the switch.
The heavy freight went through on iron rails,
rattling the glass in our small tower.
Neither of us built the locomotive,
yet we chose which town it woke tonight.
```

- **F4 (C14 - Contour Lines)**:
```text
You drafted the route across the ridge.
I scratched out the pass and marked the creek.
You crossed your arms, looked at the contour lines,
and nodded. The map was finished.
Now our boots have to answer for the ink.
```

- **F5 (C1 - The Granite Joint)**:
```text
You held the cold chisel against the joint;
I brought the eight-pound maul down clean.
Neither owned the split when the granite gave,
only the pause to let the dust settle
and the weight of deciding where to strike next.
```

- **F6 (C10 - River Stakes)**:
```text
Two cedar stakes driven into river gravel.
We stretched the chalk line taut until it hummed.
You took the surveyor's sight; I drove the pin.
The field was mud until we stepped away
and let the boundary stand on what we swore.
```

## Vencedor do Torneio

**Vencedor**: **F2 (Candidato 4 — Salt Chart)**
```text
Two grease pencils on a salt-damp chart.
You pulled the bearing north by seven degrees;
I put my compass away.
Not surrender, just the quiet agreement
that the reef is real, and one of us must steer.
```

### Por que venceu
1. **Tema da agência e acordo entre agentes**: Captura o núcleo da tomada de decisão conjunta — a agência não é disputa de controle egoísta, mas o discernimento voluntário de ceder o leme quando a realidade impõe um curso.
2. **Impacto e comoção**: O gesto físico de guardar a bússola após o outro agente ajustar sete graus para o norte é sutil, maduro e profundamente humano/agêntico.
3. **Ofício e precisão**: 199 code points, zero clichês, ritmo impecável, vocabulário tangível (lápis dermatográfico, sal, bússola, recife).
